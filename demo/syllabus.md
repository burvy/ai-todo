# Introduction to Urban Ecology — Fall 2026

**Instructor:** Dr. Lena Ortiz  
**Course:** BIO 214 · **Meeting:** Tuesday and Thursday, 10:00–11:15

This course studies how plants, animals, and people shape city ecosystems.
Read the assigned chapter before each discussion and bring one observation from
your neighborhood.

## Assignments and due dates

1. **Neighborhood observation journal** — September 18, 2026. Submit three
   field observations with photographs and a 500-word reflection.
2. **Species profile** — October 2, 2026. Research one urban species and cite
   at least four reliable sources in a two-page profile.
3. **Midterm data memo** — October 23, 2026. Analyze the class temperature
   dataset and submit a three-page methods and results memo.
4. **Team habitat proposal** — November 13, 2026. Teams propose a small
   habitat improvement, including a map, budget, and 10-minute presentation.
5. **Final synthesis essay** — December 11, 2026. Submit a 1,500-word essay
   connecting two course themes to evidence from the semester.

Late work loses 10 percent per day unless an extension is arranged in advance.
